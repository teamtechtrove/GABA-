Our Mission

AI should amplify
human potential,
not replace it.

GABA was born from a simple belief: artificial intelligence should be accessible, intelligent, and deeply personal. In a world flooded with generic AI tools, we set out to build something different — an AI assistant that doesn't just generate text, but truly understands context, reasoning, and intent.

We believe the future of AI isn't about replacing human thought — it's about amplifying it. Whether you're a student grappling with complex concepts, a developer debugging code, or a professional seeking deeper insights, GABA is designed to be your cognitive partner.

Our mission is to democratize access to advanced AI reasoning capabilities, making deep thinking, code generation, and intelligent search available to everyone — regardless of background, location, or resources.

The Origin Story

How GABA came to be

Three milestones. One relentless mission.

2024
The Spark

The idea for GABA emerged from a simple observation: existing AI tools were powerful but impersonal. They answered questions without truly understanding the person asking. We envisioned an AI that could reason deeply, explain clearly, and adapt to individual needs.

2025
The Build

Starting as a solo project in Dhaka, Bangladesh, GABA was built with relentless curiosity and a commitment to excellence. Every line of code, every prompt engineered, every interaction refined — crafted to deliver an experience that feels genuinely intelligent.

2026 & Beyond
The Vision

Today, GABA serves users worldwide who demand more from their AI. Tomorrow, we aim to build the most trusted generative brain — one that doesn't just respond, but truly comprehends, reasons, and empowers.

The Technology

What powers GABA

Deep Reasoning

Step-by-step logical analysis for complex problems. GABA doesn't just answer — it thinks through the solution, shows its work, and explains its reasoning in plain language.

Code Generation

Write, debug, and explain code in 20+ languages. From Python to JavaScript, with syntax-highlighted output, inline comments, and live execution support.

Web Search

Real-time information retrieval integrated directly into conversation. Say "search" and GABA fetches live results, synthesizes them, and responds with current, accurate information.

Multi-Model Fallback

Groq, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek in an intelligent fallback chain. If one provider is unavailable or rate-limited, GABA seamlessly routes to the next — zero downtime for you.

Contextual Memory

Conversations that remember and build upon previous messages within the session. Full history is preserved for logged-in users — your AI grows smarter with every exchange.
